About this dashboard

Purpose. This research monitor shows how solar and wind generation shares have changed relative to natural gas and coal. It supports research prioritisation; it does not assess securities or recommend investments.

Prototype status. This is a working prototype; the group's manual independent validation is pending.

Source and scope. U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), approved cleaned utility-scale generation input; monthly observations from January 2016 through July 2026, in GWh. The 2026 observations are partial and preliminary. “Other” is a residual, not a separately observed technology. Small rooftop solar is outside this utility-scale dataset.

Rebuild saved data. From the project folder, run python3 calculations/build_dashboard_data.py; the script reads only the two existing calculation reference CSVs and writes data.js.

Method. Shares divide source generation by total generation. Rolling-12 shares sum each source over 12 months and divide by the same 12-month total; annual shares use complete calendar years. Share differences are percentage points. Growth is shown as percent and absolute GWh change. The technology filter never changes the total-generation denominator.

Limitations. Generation is not capacity, revenue, profit, valuation or investment return. Weather, demand and the total-generation denominator affect shares. EIA observations can be revised. Member 1 noted that 2025 values differ from another EIA table and the difference is unexplained; this prototype consistently uses the approved input, so 2025 and 2026-vs-2025 growth are more uncertain.
