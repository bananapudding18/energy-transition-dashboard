About this dashboard

Purpose. Compare US generation trends and company price changes versus SPY to prioritise research.

Prototype status. Independent student validation completed.

Source and scope. U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), approved cleaned utility-scale generation input; monthly observations in GWh. “Other” is a residual, not a separately observed technology. Small rooftop solar is outside this utility-scale dataset.

Equity method. Price change = 100 × (end Close / start Close − 1); excess = company change − SPY change, in pp. Rebased price = 100 × Close / starting Close. Comparisons require exact, complete calendar-month endpoints; partial months are excluded and missing prices stay unavailable. Historical years select the Section 03 solar/wind theme. Its recent evidence uses consecutive 12-month generation windows and fixed 12M company/SPY comparisons at the common month. Section 02 company and lookback controls do not change that screen.

Manual equity snapshots. SPY is a broad equity-market benchmark.

Method. Shares use total generation. Rolling-12 shares divide summed source GWh by summed total GWh; annual shares use complete years. Share changes are in pp; growth uses % and GWh. Filters do not change denominators.

Limitations. Generation is not capacity, revenue, profit, valuation or investment return. Weather, demand and the total-generation denominator affect shares. EIA observations can be revised. 2025 values differ from another EIA table and the difference is unexplained; this prototype consistently uses the approved input, so 2025 and 2026-vs-2025 growth are more uncertain.

Educational project. Not investment advice.
