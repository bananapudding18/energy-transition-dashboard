About this dashboard

Purpose. This Meridian research monitor compares generation trends and selected company closing-price changes against SPY. It supports research prioritisation, not investment recommendations.

Prototype status. This is a working prototype; the group's manual independent validation is pending.

Source and scope. U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA), approved cleaned utility-scale generation input; monthly observations in GWh. “Other” is a residual, not a separately observed technology. Small rooftop solar is outside this utility-scale dataset.

Equity method. Closing-price change = 100 × (end Close / start Close − 1); excess = company change minus SPY change in percentage points. Rebased price = 100 × Close / starting Close. Exact calendar-month endpoints are required; partial months are excluded from calculations. Missing prices are unavailable, never zero. The common endpoint is available for EIA, all stocks and SPY. The research narrative compares solar and wind share changes over the selected complete years, with a separate fixed 12-month check of the solar-tagged equities against SPY. Neither generation nor price dispersion proves mispricing.

Manual equity snapshots. Adjustment basis, dividends, corporate actions and company exposure tags are unverified. SPY is a broad equity-market benchmark. Do not publicly host raw prices or derived results until exact-series access, AI use, reuse and redistribution permissions have been documented and approved.

Method. Shares divide source generation by total generation. Rolling-12 shares sum each source over 12 months and divide by the same 12-month total; annual shares use complete calendar years. Share differences are percentage points. Growth is shown as percent and absolute GWh change. The technology filter never changes the total-generation denominator.

Limitations. Generation is not capacity, revenue, profit, valuation or investment return. Weather, demand and the total-generation denominator affect shares. EIA observations can be revised. Member 1 noted that 2025 values differ from another EIA table and the difference is unexplained; this prototype consistently uses the approved input, so 2025 and 2026-vs-2025 growth are more uncertain.

Educational project. Not investment advice.
